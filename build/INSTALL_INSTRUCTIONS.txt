MAC INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS
=============================

Welcome to NullByte! 

Because this application is open-source and not distributed through the official Mac App Store or signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate, macOS Gatekeeper may flag it as "damaged", "malware", or say it "cannot be verified".

Do not panic! The app is completely safe. To bypass macOS Gatekeeper:

OPTION 1: The Terminal Method (Recommended & Easiest)
1. Drag the NullByte app into your Applications folder as usual.
2. Open the "Terminal" app on your Mac.
3. Paste the following exact command and hit Enter:
   sudo xattr -cr /Applications/NullByte.app
4. It will ask for your Mac login password. Type it (you won't see the characters as you type) and hit Enter.
5. You can now launch NullByte normally!

OPTION 2: The Right-Click Method
1. Drag the NullByte app into your Applications folder.
2. Open your Applications folder in Finder.
3. Right-click (or Control-click) on the NullByte app.
4. Select "Open" from the context menu. 
5. A warning dialog will appear. Click "Open" anyway. 
   (If this doesn't work the first time, open your Mac's System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to the NullByte block notice).
